# Solara public-alpha tester guide

Open https://solara-travel-mvp1.onrender.com. Commit 51 is a release candidate;
hosted acceptance is pending. The free service can take time to wake up.

Solara helps you discover destinations, compare seasonal fit and build an editable
trip outline. Its climate evidence describes historical seasonal conditions,
not a live forecast. Stories and images are optional; the evidence-based result
remains the authority.

1. Choose dates and try a city, country or region. Mix destinations, or leave
   them blank for worldwide discovery. Confirm suggested places after typing.
   You can select up to 15 scopes. Try interests, pace and climate preferences.
2. Review Seasonal Fit, Places to See, Seasonal Feel and any Good to Know context.
   For a required cold/snowy trip in Thailand or the Philippines, expect truthful
   zero matches and recovery choices rather than tropical substitutions.
3. Select **Build this trip**, or shortlist destinations and choose **Build a
   multi-stop trip**. Set traveller composition, pace and practical needs. Edit
   destination order and day allocations; dates follow those allocations.
4. In the studio, discover activities for the selected place. Add, remove,
   replace, move or reorder them across Morning, Afternoon and Evening. Check
   day-load guidance; loading or an unavailable activity list should preserve
   your plan. Activities keep their city and country when you edit the route.
5. Travel between stops is **unresolved** because no routing service is connected.
   Confirm travel times yourself before relying on arrival-day plans. Solara does
   not invent transport modes, schedules or durations.
6. Download the active trip as text or JSON. Practical requirements are excluded
   by default; include them only if you choose. Downloads stay local until you
   share them yourself. They are planning notes, not reservations.

The active trip lives only in the open page. There are no accounts or saved trips;
refreshing/closing can lose your edits. No trip is stored in browser storage or a
server trip database. Provider services process inputs needed for recommendations.
Keep sensitive personal details out of free-form descriptions and feedback.

Use the feedback form deliberately. Describe what you tried, what you expected,
what happened and the displayed request reference if available. A screenshot and
device/browser details can help when reporting a defect to the owner; remove
private information. Test keyboard navigation, mobile layout and reduced motion.
If a provider is unavailable or a cooldown appears, follow the visible guidance.

Do not expect booking, hotel/activity/transport inventory, live prices, payments,
accounts, saved trips, a routing provider, visa/legal advice or a RAG knowledge
base. Confirm travel, entry and safety information with authoritative sources.
